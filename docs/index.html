Controls

  • Reveal: click or tap. Flag: right-click or long-press, or switch on flag mode.
  • Open around a number whose flags are all placed: double-click or double-tap it, or middle-click.
  • New game: the face. Stats: best times, win rate and streaks for each board, kept in this browser.

The assist

Press Assist when you are stuck. If no hidden cell is provably safe from what you can see (the numbers and the mine count), it opens a safe cell for you, next to the numbers when it can, so no game ever needs a guess. If a safe cell could be deduced, pressing it loses the game and shows that cell in green. Flags don't count as knowledge: a wrong flag can't trick it.

The rules are written as laws and machine-checked in Bend 2: see LAWS.bend.
